# K8 Canvas Canonical Epic

## Epic story

As the sole owner of K8 Canvas, I want an intelligent, open and evidence-led
WordPress building system that creates high-quality websites without unnecessary
complexity, builder lock-in or preventable technical debt, so every approved
capability contributes directly to a usable and commercially defensible product.

## Product position

K8 Canvas is a **website quality and ownership system**, not another collection of
page-builder widgets.

It brings together four concerns that competing products commonly separate:

1. visual construction;
2. quality governance;
3. visibility intelligence; and
4. open ownership.

## Current authority model

- Anthony Xavier is the sole product owner and final decision authority.
- The K8 GPT workforce may research, specify, implement, review and validate only
  within the active approved story.
- GPT specialisation does not confer ownership, publishing authority or commercial
  authority.
- PBAC, customer accounts and delegated publishing are deferred until the approved
  commercial-access gate.

## Product guardrails

- WordPress remains authoritative for content, revisions and rendered site state.
- Python analyses and recommends; it does not autonomously publish.
- New capabilities must be approachable, testable and traceable to this Epic.
- Clean semantic output and graceful exit are product requirements.
- Accessibility, responsiveness, visibility and performance are build-time
  concerns, not optional audits added after publication.
- No story is active merely because it appears in a roadmap or backlog.

## Epic success condition

K8 Canvas reaches its first product proof when an owner can construct a real
WordPress page from governed components, receive explainable Python analysis,
correct material defects, publish intentionally and retain usable content if the
Canvas layer is removed.
